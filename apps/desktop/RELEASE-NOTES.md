**A garden for your journal.** **🌱 Garden** in the menu grows a plant for every entry you write, in a flower bed for each month. The longer the entry, the bigger the plant, from a little sprout to a flowering tree, and its mood picks the flower (😄 a sunflower, 🥰 a rose, no mood a wildflower). Tap a plant to open its entry.

**15 rarity tiers.** Writing earns your garden Growth, which takes it from 🌱 Common through Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, Radiant, Prismatic, Crystal, Celestial, Aurora, Cosmic, Eternal and Primordial to 🌳 Worldbloom, and then ★ rings. Each new plant blooms at your garden's tier, and entries of 600 or 1,200 words bloom one or two tiers higher. Rarer plants get dew, gold, flames, rainbows, crystals, halos and a crown. Growth comes from the days you write: copied text, links and filler don't count, and plants never wilt. Your existing entries are planted straight away.

**Perks.** Every tier unlocks a perk in the **Garden shed**: petal celebrations, visiting butterflies, colours for the whole app, a sky that follows the time of day, paths, lanterns, bed borders, a gate ornament, starry, aurora and nebula skies, and more. Nothing changes until you choose it, and nothing locks again. Don't want a garden? Turn it off in **Settings → Appearance**.

What came in 0.5.0:

**Printed books are gone.** Logbook no longer makes or sells printed books: the **Book** tab is removed. Everything else stays, including **Export** (on an entry, the journal page, search results, or **Settings → Export**): your entries as Markdown, plain text, a web page or a PDF you can print yourself. Book drafts saved by earlier versions are deleted from your journal.

**From 0.4.1:** writing prompts on new entries (**Write about this**, **🔀 Another**; off in **Settings → Appearance**).

What came in 0.4.0:

**Music from a link.** 🎵 Music on an entry now takes a link as well as a sound file: **🔗 Paste a link…** and press **Play**. YouTube videos and live streams (Lofi Girl, say), Vimeo, Rumble, Dailymotion, Twitch, Kick and Streamable play in the site's own player, right there in the music bar, ads and all (you can skip or mute them with the player's buttons). A link to a sound file or an internet radio station plays like a sound file. If a site won't let its video play inside Logbook, the bar says so and offers to open it in your browser. Links need the internet.

**Fixed:** on phones, the buttons on an entry's cover no longer run off the edge of the screen; they scroll sideways. The flag picker lists each country once, and only flags Logbook can draw (from 0.3.1 and 0.3.2).

**Update every copy of Logbook you use.** Once 0.4.0 opens your journal, earlier versions won't open it (they'd lose music links when saving), and offer their update instead. On a computer, Logbook updates itself (**Settings → Updates**); on Android, install the new `.apk` over the old one.

What came in 0.3.0:

**Logbook for Android.** Logbook now comes as an Android app (Android 7 or later): the file ending in `.apk` below. Your journal is kept inside the app on your phone; move it between phone and computer with a backup file (**Settings → Backups**). It isn't on Google Play: open the release page on your phone, tap the `.apk`, and allow your browser to install apps when Android asks. Later versions install over it.

**Richer writing.** The toolbar over your text now has headings 1 to 6 (as many as you like), underline, strikethrough, colours and highlights, eight fonts, alignment, **checklists with tick boxes**, **tables**, quotes and code, and a picker for **country flags** 🇯🇵, which now show on every computer, Windows too. **Settings → Appearance → Easier reading** sets the text size, line spacing and the font you write in, including Atkinson Hyperlegible and OpenDyslexic.

**A clock.** **⏰ Clock** in the menu has alarms (once, or on the days you choose), a timer and a stopwatch with laps. They ring on any page, even with the journal locked, with Snooze and Stop. On Android they ring even when Logbook is closed; on a computer, while Logbook is open.

**Also new**

- **Background music:** 🎵 Music gives an entry a sound file that plays while it's open.
- **Entries that expire:** ⏳ Expiry moves an entry to the trash by itself at a time you choose (you can still restore it for 30 days).
- **Export anywhere:** ⬇️ Export on an entry, on the journal page and on search results.

- Windows: "Windows protected your PC" → **More info** → **Run anyway**.
