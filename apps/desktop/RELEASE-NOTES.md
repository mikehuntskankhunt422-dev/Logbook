**0.3.1 fixes the flag picker**, which listed some countries twice (Germany, France, the United Kingdom, Russia and ten more), the second time with a flag that showed as two letters. Each country is listed once now.

What came in 0.3.0:

**Logbook for Android.** Logbook now comes as an Android app (Android 7 or later): the file ending in `.apk` below. Your journal is kept inside the app on your phone; move it between phone and computer with a backup file (**Settings → Backups**). It isn't on Google Play: open the release page on your phone, tap the `.apk`, and allow your browser to install apps when Android asks. Later versions install over it.

**Richer writing.** The toolbar over your text now has headings 1 to 6 (as many as you like), underline, strikethrough, colours and highlights, eight fonts, alignment, **checklists with tick boxes**, **tables**, quotes and code, and a picker for **country flags** 🇯🇵, which now show on every computer, Windows too. **Settings → Appearance → Easier reading** sets the text size, line spacing and the font you write in, including Atkinson Hyperlegible and OpenDyslexic.

**A clock.** **⏰ Clock** in the menu has alarms (once, or on the days you choose), a timer and a stopwatch with laps. They ring on any page, even with the journal locked, with Snooze and Stop. On Android they ring even when Logbook is closed; on a computer, while Logbook is open.

**Also new**

- **Background music:** 🎵 Music gives an entry a sound file that plays while it's open.
- **Entries that expire:** ⏳ Expiry moves an entry to the trash by itself at a time you choose (you can still restore it for 30 days).
- **Export anywhere:** ⬇️ Export on an entry, on the journal page and on search results.
- Smoother pages, dialogs and menus, with less movement when your system asks for reduced motion.

**Important if you use Logbook on more than one computer:** once this version opens your journal, older versions (0.2.0) won't open it, so they can't lose your new formatting. Update Logbook on each computer; 0.2.0 offers the update itself.

**Download** the file for your device below:

- **Windows 10 or 11:** the file ending in `_x64-setup.exe`
- **macOS 10.15 or later** (Apple silicon and Intel): the file ending in `_universal.dmg`
- **Linux:** the `.AppImage` (most distributions), `.deb` (Ubuntu, Debian) or `.rpm` (Fedora)
- **Android 7 or later:** the file ending in `.apk`

Already on 0.2.0? Logbook updates itself: **Update and restart** when it asks, or **Settings → Updates**.

**The first time you open it:** Logbook isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, so your computer warns about it once.

- Windows: "Windows protected your PC" → **More info** → **Run anyway**.
- macOS 15 or later: drag Logbook to Applications and open it. When macOS says it can't check it, click **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. On earlier versions, Control-click Logbook in Applications and choose **Open**.
- Linux AppImage: `chmod +x Logbook_*.AppImage`, then run it.

**Not in this preview yet:** ordering printed books (the book builder and its preview work; ordering opens in an update), and a screen for sorting out conflicting copies made by a sync service.

This is a preview: keep a backup of anything you'd hate to lose (**Settings → Backups**). Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
